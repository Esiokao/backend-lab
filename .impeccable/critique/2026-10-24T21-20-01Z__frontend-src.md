## Design Specificity Verdict

**6/10 — the skin is authored, the layout is generic.** Title blocks ("SHEET STORE-01", "REV A"), stamp badges, dimension lines on dashboard stat cards, and the sketch grid are genuinely Vellum and couldn't be pasted onto a SaaS admin. But the layout underneath is a default sidebar+cards template that never uses drafting-sheet grammar: no column rulings, no baseline rhythm, no registration of the grid to the "sheet". The user's instinct (「版面不夠好看」) is correct — the world is drawn on the furniture, not on the floor plan.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI detector on frontend/src: 0 findings, all rules clean. Browser overlay evidence: catalog 39 findings (38 undersized-ui-text down to 9px, 1 cream-palette note); Edit product dialog 46 (adds 10px form labels, 4.3:1 advisory contrast, skipped-heading h1→h3); cart 2; admin dashboard 33 (8.4px "GET /stats", 5 table headers at 4.2:1, one genuine 2.6:1 fail #5f7460 on #2a3226, repeating-stripes-gradient on body). Dark theme: 0 findings — all issues are light-theme-specific.

## Overall Impression

The Vellum identity is real and the checkout-result moment is genuinely good, but the layout execution undersells it: a generic rail+grid with no drafting grammar, an unscaled type ramp that makes everything look cheap-small, and a storefront with no product pages. Fixing the type/spacing scale and adding column rulings would do more for 「版面不夠好看」 than any palette work.

## What's Working

1. **Title-block page headings** (Storefront/CART-01/CHECKOUT-01 with SHEET + REV cells) — consistent, thematic, instantly authored.
2. **Dashboard stat cards with dimension-line annotations** ("7 today |—| GET/stats") — the best fusion of data and metaphor on the site.
3. **Cart summary hierarchy** — "Selected / 2 items · cart total $79.00" with 22px mono total is a correct focal point.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Product cards are dead ends.** No detail view/route exists (StorefrontView.vue only opens a modal for admin create/edit); clicking a product does nothing. Fix: add `/product/:id` (or detail modal), make image+name clickable, show full name/description/big image.
- **[P0] No type/spacing scale — everything looks cheap-small.** design.css uses 9px stamps, 10px labels, 11px prices, 12.5px product names; gaps of 2/6/7/8/9/10/11/13/14/17/22/23px. Fix: 4px spacing scale (4/8/12/16/24/32/48), type scale 12/14/16/20/28 with body min 14px; product name to 14–15px with two-line clamp; kill the 9px tag-stamp.
- **[P1] Catalog grid composition wastes the sheet.** 212px rail + 28px gap leaves a 940px grid of 3×304px cards at 14px gutters (cramped); the 320px search box floats alone, left-aligned, with dead space right; the rail ends early leaving an empty left column. Fix: grid gap 20–24px; search full-width of the tools row aligned to grid right edge or paired with a sort select; give the rail a full-height vertical ruling line (drafting-sheet column line).
- **[P1] Cart table doesn't collapse on mobile.** At 390px it's a 5-column table with horizontal scroll hiding QTY/SUBTOTAL/remove. Fix: <760px switch to stacked row cards (checkbox, name+price, stepper, line total, ✕) and add product thumbnails.
- **[P2] No footer anywhere.** Storefront stops after the last card. Fix: colophon footer with title-block treatment ("SHEET 01 · Backend Lab · drawn 2026") linking About/Orders/Account.

## Questions to Consider

1. If every product is stamped "NEW IN", is the stamp a badge or wallpaper — what would a Vellum revision stamp *do* (e.g., a real "revised" date per product)?
2. The dashboard earns the metaphor; the storefront borrows it. Would a column-grid ruling (visible vertical rules bounding rail/grid, like a real drafting sheet) fix the "not good-looking" complaint more than any palette tweak?
3. Can a shop with no product pages be called a storefront — or is Add-to-cart-only a feature you're willing to defend?
